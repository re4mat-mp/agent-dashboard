What the board shows, for people and for AI. Every count is computed from crew/.

A board for a crew of 7 Claude Code agents, each with one craft, and the Lead who sends them work. All data is invented. It comes from 52 XP awards (16,200 XP in total) and 84 dispatches. The newest entry in either log is from 2025-04-25.

The Lead: Artisan, level 10, 2,314 XP. The Lead never earns XP: it is the crew's 16,200 XP divided by 7.

  1. Smith (builder): Master, level 15, 5,200 XP from 14 awards, dispatched 19 times. Writes the change, runs it, and shows it working before calling it done.
  2. Skeptic (reviewer): Artisan, level 14, 4,300 XP from 12 awards, dispatched 12 times. Reads every change it did not write and tries to break it.
  3. Cartographer (analyst): Artisan, level 10, 2,300 XP from 7 awards, dispatched 14 times. Turns a pile of feedback into a ranked map of what hurts most.
  4. Keeper (notekeeper): Journeyman, level 9, 1,900 XP from 7 awards, dispatched 10 times. Keeps the notes tidy: what we know, where a fact belongs, what went stale.
  5. Scribe (writer): Journeyman, level 7, 900 XP from 4 awards, dispatched 13 times. Drafts the words a person will read: a summary, a release note, a reply.
  6. Watch (operations): Journeyman, level 8, 1,300 XP from 5 awards, dispatched 8 times. Notices when something that should have run did not, and finds out why.
  7. Assayer (data): Apprentice, level 4, 300 XP from 3 awards, dispatched 8 times. Runs the numbers and says how sure it is about each one.

Hand-offs, a craft sending work to another craft: 32. The most frequent: Cartographer to Smith (6), Skeptic to Smith (5), Smith to Keeper (5).

Rank-ups not yet marked as watched in crew/reveals_seen.json: 1 (Cartographer reached Artisan). With JavaScript on, each one plays once as a pop-up.

The curve: XP to reach level L = 25 x (L - 1)^2. Ranks start at level 1 (Apprentice), 5 (Journeyman), 10 (Artisan), 15 (Master), 20 (Grandmaster).
